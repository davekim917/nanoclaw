- 2026-10-06 · #1463 · borrowed predicate · A user-facing status line was derived from the predicate that decides whether to queue a recovery wake. That predicate answers whether another wake would be redundant, and three of its states do not mean the session comes back: a continuation whose recovery budget is spent suppresses a wake and resumes nothing, a closed or archived session can hold queued rows it will never take, and the writer's own queued row is deliberately excluded from suppression although it does resume the session. Each was a separate finding at a separate line, and patching the three sites would have left the next state to be found the same way.
